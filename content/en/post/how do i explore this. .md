## So is this how I edit ?
I think often about what it is to learn a language without the proper syntax. 
My foray into building a site is vaguely simillar to how I see myself learning Kannada. 
Stringing words like a caveman and hope that the person on the other end understands what I am trying to say. Unlike the warmth of the human who lives with me. My computer is far less forgiving. The Internet, is worse. 
<br>
I must learn its ways if I am to somehow learn to dump all that's in my mind to an unseeing, unhearing and uncaring void. How else will I metamorphose?


It is odd that the godzilla within. The same One that I hope will burst out of this flesh it treats a cocoon, By being unfunny on the internet.
