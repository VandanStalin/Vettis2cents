## The beauty of the things I cant capture.

Adhavan smokes in the kitchen, 
Sunlight filters through an empty exhaust vent, as he talks to me, I gaze up in wonder at the wisp of smoke caught in the morning sun. 
Oh the beauty of the things i can't capture. 

I look at pictures of a lake i used to visit almost every week. 
I capture the glimmer of something gorgeous, in the union of the shadow that my boat casts, and the rays of sunlight caught in the wake leave. 
glimmers.

A beauty only I can see, in a vision I can't quite comprehend. 
I lose track of what my partner is talking about as I am too absorbed trying to understand what it is that i am processing. 

In the corner of my eye, i notice something moving inside the latch of my bathroom door. 
One part of me thinks that i've finally lost it, that It is my mind conjuring up images to compensate for the stillness of my life. 
The other, believes that it is the antaennae of a cockroach that we have failed to kill(We've had encounters with a few uninvited guests, these past few days)

Morbid curiosity overwhelms me. 
Midway through my shower, I give in. 
With baited breath, I slowly push the latch up, expecting the worst. 

What awaits me is an 8 legged critted, perched perfectly atop the lock. it, adjusts its legs to fit the top better almost as though to camoflauge itself. It does a good job I think to myself. 

I wonder, what a birdseye view of the bathroom means to a spider that is nearsighted.

The spider now resides in a corner of the door frame near indistingushable from a blemish, as it wonders the eldritch horrors far beyond it's comprehension.
