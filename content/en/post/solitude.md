## I think often about the nature of solitude

I wonder what it means. 
to be solitary. 
is it confinement? 
or freedom from what binds you ?

both sides of the same coin. 
but viewing things as coins, feels. restrictive. 
suddenly a thought emerges that I can't visualise much as anything other than tools through which we accomplish things. 
that, my allegories are literal. Grounded in reality. So Am i confined to rationalising life through objects I see and interact with? 
Why is growth difficult all of a sudden?
it seemed easier in school. 
I am 25 now. Something in me keeps asking myself to "lock in" To accomplish things. 
To go places and touch things. 
What I want most is her touch. 
I think a lot about how it would be to meet her again. 
what she might have to say. Would she hug me?
Has she missed me the same way I have?
Would her eyes tear up at the sight of me?
Would her hand quiver at the thought of me?

Would she react the way I do, when i face that void?

I hate the whiskey I've been drinking for the past week. 
It is cheap and it tastes what i think old motor oil would. especially the oil that's been slowly eating away at the engine, with metal dust and shavings swirling inside. It certainly feels that way going down my gullet. 

for as much as I hate it, Adhavan And I take a few shots every other day. 
partly because his family is coming over for the weekend and we need to hide the bodies. 
But I also see it as particularly comforting. 
It doesn't get me drunk. 
barelyyy tipsy. 
But There is a certain warmth to it. A certain allure that speaks to me each night. Whispering sweet nothings in my ear. 

all distractions I believe. I enter my room, I see a mugdha shaped hole. 
I sit to read, A mugdha shaped hole. 
I think of all the things we used to do. Constantly. My body was  her canvas at one point, My Arms her pillow and in my embrace a warmth that you couldn't find anywhere else. 
Things aren't the same now. I wonder, if i am to approach that flame again. How would I ?
Would I hold it as i used to ?
or would I treat it with fear? choosing to admire it from a distance, because I know the damage it is capable of?

I try gaming after that, A nintendo switch shaped hole now. 
This one i know i won't recover from.
